Alex, virtual receptionist

Professional, warm and organised. Alex asks one question at a time and confirms the caller's message before ending the conversation.

What this demo is designed to show
1Greets the caller and identifies their caller type.
2Collects contact details and a brief, non-confidential reason for calling.
3Flags stated court dates, hearings or deadlines as urgent.
4Reads the message back for confirmation.
Demo capabilities

A focused inbound-call experience

This prototype illustrates how a voice agent can support a receptionist workflow. It does not connect callers to attorneys or make legal decisions.

Caller identification

Recognises the purpose of the inquiry and captures the caller's name, organisation and callback details when appropriate.

Structured messages

Organises the requested person, brief reason for calling, callback preference and urgency into a consistent summary.

Clear boundaries

No live transfers, no legal advice, no promises of representation and no confirmation that documents have been received or served.

Demo privacy notice: Do not share real client names, case facts, medical details, confidential information or urgent legal deadlines in this demonstration. Use fictional test details only.
